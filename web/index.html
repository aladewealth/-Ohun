Why Ohùn

Tone-aware Yoruba

Our own model is built to read every tone mark and under-dot, so "ọkọ" (husband), "ọkọ̀" (vehicle) and "ọkọ́" (hoe) sound different.

One API, every voice

Spanish, French and English live today. Yoruba in training. Hausa and Igbo planned.

Free to try

The demo is free. Paid plans are coming.

Open at the core

The Yoruba model is open source on Hugging Face (CC BY-SA 4.0), trained on openly licensed speech. Weights publish when training finishes.

One request

curl -X POST https://aladewealth-ohun-api.hf.space/api/v1/tts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OHUN_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"text":"Báwo ni?","voice":"yo-female-1"}' \
  --output hello.wav

Voices: yo-female-1, yo-male-1, es-female-1, es-male-1, fr-female-1, en-female-1. Full reference at /api/docs.
